# Sit Break v0.6.0

**English** | [简体中文](v0.6.0.md)

Scheduled timer now switches rounds by the computer’s local clock. For a 09:00–12:00 period with 55 minutes of work and 5 minutes of rest per round, opening the app at 10:20 joins round 2. Round 3 starts automatically at 11:00.

## Scheduled Timer

- The timer joins the current round and phase when you start late, reopen the app, or wake the computer. Expired break reminders are skipped.
- Starting a break late gives you the time left in its scheduled window. Snoozing or ending a break early leaves the next round at its scheduled time.
- Pause reminders hides and silences reminders while rounds keep following the clock. Auto-reset when away applies to Simple timer.
- Draft plan shows unsaved changes. Current progress shows the active round and the saved upcoming rounds.

## Upgrading and Compatibility

Quit the old version before replacing it. The app keeps your existing regular-release settings, schedules, and manual pause choice. It recalculates the current round and remaining time from the local clock. Preview builds store settings separately.

The app requires macOS 12 or later with a system WebView that supports WebKit 15.4+, or Windows 10/11 x64 with WebView2 based on Chromium 108 or later. The macOS app uses ad-hoc signing and the Windows installers are unsigned. See the [user guide](../../README.md) and [schedule guide](../schedules.en.md) for installation and schedule setup.
