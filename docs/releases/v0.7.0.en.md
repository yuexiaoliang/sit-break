# Sit Break v0.7.0

**English** | [简体中文](v0.7.0.md)

If a reminder or break window covers something you need, drag the blank space along its edge or between its contents to move it. This also works when a local video is playing. The next pop-up opens at the bottom right of its screen.

- Drag the “Sit Break” title bar at the top of Settings to move the window.
- The schedule guide adds a [workday break example](../schedules.en.md#workday-break-example) with a lunch gap, text reminders, and guidance from a local video.

Thanks to [wurara7](https://github.com/wurara7) for the initial window dragging contribution in [PR #29](https://github.com/yuexiaoliang/sit-break/pull/29).

## Upgrading and Compatibility

Quit any running regular or preview builds before upgrading. The app keeps your existing regular-release settings, schedules, and manual pause choice. Preview builds store settings separately.

The app requires macOS 12 or later with a system WebView that supports WebKit 15.4+, or Windows 10/11 x64 with WebView2 based on Chromium 108 or later. The macOS app uses ad-hoc signing and the Windows installers are unsigned. See the [user guide](../../README.md) and [schedule guide](../schedules.en.md) for installation and setup.
