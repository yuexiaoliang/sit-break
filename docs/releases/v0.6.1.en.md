# Sit Break v0.6.1

**English** | [简体中文](v0.6.1.md)

In Scheduled timer, selecting Take a break starts the full configured countdown. If a five-minute break is due at 09:55 and you start it at 09:57, it runs until 10:02. The timer then rejoins the schedule at the current time.

- A break in progress can finish across a round boundary, the end of a time period, or midnight. Later reminders keep their scheduled times.
- Reopening the app or waking the computer resumes any break still in progress. Current progress shows the time left on that break.

## Upgrading and Compatibility

Quit the old version before replacing it. The app keeps your existing regular-release settings, schedules, and manual pause choice. Breaks started after upgrading use the full configured duration. Preview builds store settings separately.

The app requires macOS 12 or later with a system WebView that supports WebKit 15.4+, or Windows 10/11 x64 with WebView2 based on Chromium 108 or later. The macOS app uses ad-hoc signing and the Windows installers are unsigned. See the [user guide](../../README.md) and [schedule guide](../schedules.en.md) for installation and schedule setup.
