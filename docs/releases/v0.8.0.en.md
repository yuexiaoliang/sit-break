# Sit Break v0.8.0

**English** | [简体中文](v0.8.0.md)

The macOS menu bar now shows one icon and a short status. During work, the simple timer shows “Work,” and the scheduled timer shows the current round. The rounded background fill shrinks from right to left as time runs down. Click the item to see the exact countdown.

- Pausing the simple timer switches to a pause icon and freezes the gray progress fill.
- Pausing scheduled reminders switches to a muted bell while progress continues. After you finish a break, the item shows “Done” for the rest of that round’s scheduled break window.
- The background fits the system menu bar item and uses the same rounded outline when pressed and released.

## Upgrading and Compatibility

Quit any running regular or preview builds before upgrading. The app keeps your existing regular-release settings, schedules, and manual pause choice. Preview builds store settings separately.

The app requires macOS 12 or later with a system WebView that supports WebKit 15.4+, or Windows 10/11 x64 with WebView2 based on Chromium 108 or later. The macOS app uses ad-hoc signing and the Windows installers are unsigned. See the [user guide](../../README.md) and [schedule guide](../schedules.en.md) for installation and setup.
